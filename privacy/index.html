Privacy Policy

Website

When making an account on the www.mioclient.me website, we collect your username, email and your (hashed) password.
When purchasing access to our software, we collect your unique payment ID which is given to us by our payment providers.
Depending on the chosen payment method our payment providers may collect:
 1. The blockchain transaction ID
 2. Your name, email, payment method information and the date of purchase

Software

When running our software, the following end user information is being processed:
 1. Processor
 2. Graphics card
 3. RAM size
 4. OS disk serial number
 5. OS user name
 6. IP address
 7. Mio authentication token

The computer information gets hashed and is used as your hardware ID for authentication.

Deletion

If you wish to delete your personal information from our resources, you can do so by contacting us.
